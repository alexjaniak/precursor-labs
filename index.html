Precursor Labs is a research company studying the organizing principles and infrastructure for collective intelligence.

We believe the next generation of autonomous systems will need to coordinate with others, reason in dynamic environments, and allocate resources under uncertainty. We are engineers, financiers, and company builders focusing on three areas:

Multi-Agent Systems

Why do specialized AI systems dominate stationary environments, while frontier LLMs still struggle with dynamic social tasks like negotiation, sales, persuasion, investing, and fundraising? What training paradigm would close that gap?

AI/Compute Market Structure

How do capital markets, compute, energy, GPU depreciation and project finance shape the rate and direction of AI development? How does practical marginal utility shape the quantity and quality of deployed models?

Market Design and Governance

How can verification, trust and market design shape coordination between humans, agents, and their social structures?

We are especially interested in partners working on interactive learning, multi-agent RL, inference infrastructure, compute finance and market design.
