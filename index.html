Precursor Labs

Precursor Labs is a research company studying the organizing principles and infrastructure for collective intelligence.

We believe the next generation of autonomous systems will need to coordinate with others, reason in dynamic environments, and allocate resources under uncertainty.

We are engineers, financiers, and company builders focusing on three areas:

Multi agent systems

Why do specialized AI systems dominate fixed environments, while frontier LLMs still struggle with dynamic social tasks like negotiation, sales, persuasion, investing, and fundraising? What training paradigm would close that gap?

AI buildout and market structure

How do capital markets, compute, energy, GPU depreciation and project finance shape the rate and direction of AI development?

Decision-making infrastructure

How can verification, trust, reward modeling and market design shape coordination between humans, agents, and organizations?

We are especially interested in partners working on interactive learning, multi-agent RL, AI infrastructure, compute finance and market design.
